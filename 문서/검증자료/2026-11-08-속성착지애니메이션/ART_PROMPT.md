# 착지 이미지 생성 조건

2026-11-08. `imagegen` 스킬의 기본 내장 이미지 생성 도구를 사용했다. 별도 API·CLI는 사용하지 않았다. 결과를 `Assets/Sprites/Effects/ElementLanding/LandingAnimationAtlas.png`로 복사하고 Unity에서 36개 Sprite로 분리했다. 요청 해상도는 1536×1536, 실제 출력은 1254×1254이며 실제 셀 크기에 맞춰 연결했다. 아래는 생성 프롬프트다.

```text
Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: production 2D GAME VFX SPRITE SHEET, transparent background. Create a SQUARE 1536x1536 sprite atlas in an EXACT UNIFORM 6 COLUMNS by 6 ROWS grid (36 equal square cells), no gutters, no labels, no lines, no characters, no floor, no shadows, no words, no UI. Every cell is 256x256 conceptual pixels and contains only a centered isolated small cartoon VFX drawing with at least 16px empty padding inside its cell. Crisp alpha edges. Completely transparent background, not checkerboard. Keep each effect registered to the exact CENTER of each cell. Soft hand-crafted flat cartoon game sprites with clean colored edges and simple inner highlights; not realistic, not pixel art. Minimal bloom, no diffuse glow. A restrained cute mobile fantasy game aesthetic.

Rows 1–5 are FIVE DIFFERENT 6-FRAME ONE-SHOT LANDING ANIMATIONS. Time runs LEFT TO RIGHT across all six cells. All are a 2D frontal game view with flattened elliptical ground effects (roughly half as high as wide), camera NEVER changes, effect centered at same point throughout. Each frame must be an individual complete effect, not fragments. Initial appearance, expansion, readable impact, settling, smaller remnants, final wisps. Shape changes and growth should progress clearly. There is no slime or character in any cell. Keep center region mostly open because an actual character will be rendered over it.

ROW 1 FIRE: six sequential frames of two or three small orange and yellow flame tongues springing up at the left and right of the contact point, curling upward, shortening into embers and vanishing. Silhouette is pointed fire; warm orange contour, yellow inner flame. No circular fire wall.
ROW 2 WATER: six sequential frames of two thin cyan elliptical ground ripples, starting small, expanding out into clean medium-wide ellipses and fading into broken thin arcs. Some tiny droplets at sides at middle frames. White-cyan highlight, transparent clear gaps. No filled blue disk.
ROW 3 EARTH: six sequential frames of a LOW wide pale ochre dust impact cloud with a few angular brown pebble accents and short broken scuff strokes on the ground, dust rolling out, puff edges changing and dispersing to little remnants. Natural irregular left/right balance. No giant rocks, no lightning-shaped cracks, no full opaque circle.
ROW 4 WIND: six sequential frames of 2–3 tapered mint-white curved gust strokes curling in an OPEN asymmetric spiral over the ground, shifting direction and thinning out as they move outward. Clear background gaps, fluid curved shapes. No complete rune circles or solid disk.
ROW 5 LIGHTNING: six sequential frames of 3–4 SHORT bright yellow angular lightning branches skimming along the ground from the contact center, initially short, rapidly extending in the first three frames, flickering into broken separated segments in frame four, receding to small sparks in frame five, only tiny remnants in frame six. Intentionally authored zigzags with a few forks, strong crisp gold-yellow contour and pale yellow core. Branches form an organic horizontally spreading footprint, not radially symmetric, not a star, not a magic seal, not random spaghetti. No purple. Keep central contact shape tiny.

ROW 6: six independent reusable PARTICLE SHAPES, NOT animation. Cell1 a small rounded beige dust puff; cell2 a single pointed orange flame/ember with yellow core; cell3 one rounded cyan water droplet with simple white highlight; cell4 one chunky angular small ochre stone with a shadow facet; cell5 one short tapered mint-white curved wind stroke; cell6 one tiny sharp golden spark. Each centered and isolated.

Critical constraints: exactly 6 columns and 6 rows, identical grid cell dimensions, one registered drawing in each cell, all artwork fits its own cell, no objects crossing cells. No stylized frame borders. No checkerboard baked into image. No backgrounds of any kind. Flat 2D sprite rendering appropriate for a light mint tile arena. Avoid ornate MMO decoration, photorealism, heavy blur, lens flare, extremely thin unreadable lines, dense micro-details. Each cell must remain readable at small in-game size.
```

이 프롬프트는 이미지 제작 조건이며 게임 규칙이 아니다. 검토 시안의 슬라임·바닥은 포함하지 않고 효과만 생성했다. 실제 적용은 기본 파티클 재질의 알파 합성·프레임 혼합과 Native Particle System 설정으로 처리했다.
